# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Quisum contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community members must make participation safe and respectful. Expected behavior includes empathy, constructive technical disagreement, consent around personal topics, respect for names and boundaries, and care with the unusually sensitive domain this project serves.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, stalking, doxxing, publishing another person's private material, pressuring anyone to disclose personal history, presenting speculation as diagnosis, or using project spaces to profile a person without consent. Never post real vaults, conversations, credentials, crisis disclosures, or identifying screenshots.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, events, private project communication, and public representation of the project. It also applies when handling vulnerability, safety, or conduct reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful. Decisions should consider impact, preserve reporter privacy, and avoid retaliation. A maintainer who is involved in a report must recuse themselves when another maintainer is available.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's private contact channel. If that channel is unavailable or the owner is implicated, use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not put sensitive evidence in a public issue. Reports will be acknowledged within seven days when the project has an active maintainer; no outcome or confidentiality can be guaranteed by third-party platforms.

## Enforcement guidelines

Responses may include:

1. A private correction for an isolated, low-impact lapse.
2. A warning and temporary participation limits for repeated or material harm.
3. Removal from project spaces for sustained harassment, threats, privacy violations, retaliation, or other severe conduct.

Maintainers will document decisions privately to the minimum extent necessary and will not use a conduct process to suppress good-faith security or safety criticism.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html) and adapted for a privacy-sensitive self-inquiry project.
