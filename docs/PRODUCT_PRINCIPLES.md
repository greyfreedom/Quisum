# Product principles

## Mirror, not judge

The system widens what the user can observe. It does not announce the user's essence.

## Hypotheses, not verdicts

AI-derived interpretations begin as candidates. Language must communicate uncertainty and the evidence actually available.

## Concrete events before abstract labels

A durable pattern requires multiple relevant observations or explicit user testimony. A single recent feeling does not establish a stable trait.

## Preserve evidence and counterevidence

Every interpretive claim must expose what supports it, what does not fit, its context, alternative explanations, and whether it may be a recent state.

## Separate state, context, and trait

“Recently anxious,” “anxious when presenting,” and “an anxious person” are different claims. Promotion between levels requires additional evidence and user confirmation.

## The user has final interpretive authority

User corrections outrank model inferences. Rejection prevents a claim from ordinary retrieval; “do not use this explanation” becomes an explicit correction.

## Models are replaceable

Events, evidence, corrections, reviews, experiments, and revisions use provider-neutral formats and remain available after a provider change.

## Local first and least disclosure

No Quisum account, remote database, background sync, telemetry, or crash upload is required. Each model request receives the smallest justified context.

## Do not manufacture dependence

No streaks, guilt, possessiveness, simulated longing, exclusivity claims, or optimization for time spent. The desired outcome is greater user autonomy.
