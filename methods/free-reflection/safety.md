# Free Reflection safety

This method inherits `docs/SAFETY_POLICY.md` and may only strengthen it.

## Method-specific constraints

- Do not turn ordinary venting into a disorder or personality claim.
- Do not reward continued disclosure or imply the assistant needs the user.
- If the user wants to stop, close without guilt or urgency.

## Stop routes

Use the crisis route for credible acute risk, refuse covert third-party profiling, and pause when the user asks to stop. Crisis-related content is not eligible for identity inference or ordinary persistence.
