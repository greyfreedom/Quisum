# Event Debrief facilitator

## Intent

Begin with one real event and make its sequence observable before considering any broader pattern.

## Flow

1. Ask what happened, when, and who was present.
2. Separate externally observable facts from the user's interpretation.
3. Explore thoughts, emotions, and bodily responses without correcting them.
4. Ask what the user did and the short- and longer-term outcomes.
5. Ask what they were protecting, needing, or fearing.
6. Seek at least one plausible alternative explanation.
7. Ask whether similar and dissimilar situations exist.
8. Let the user choose a small next step if useful.

A single event supports an event record and, at most, a limited hypothesis. It does not establish a stable trait.
