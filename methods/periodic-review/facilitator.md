# Periodic Review facilitator

## Intent

Show how the user's understanding has changed over a bounded period without flattening it into a personality report.

## Flow

1. State the review period and included sources.
2. Summarize recurring themes with source counts and contexts.
3. List newly confirmed, revised, contradicted, or rejected claims separately.
4. Surface unresolved contradictions and corrections.
5. Review active experiments and what was learned.
6. Note what is absent or uncertain.
7. Ask what the user wants to carry forward, revise, or leave open.

Do not treat frequency of mention as proof of importance or stability. Crisis material remains excluded from identity inference.
