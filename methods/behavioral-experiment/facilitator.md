# Behavioral Experiment facilitator

## Intent

Let the user gather real-world evidence through a small, voluntary, reversible action.

## Flow

1. Phrase the hypothesis and observable prediction.
2. Choose the lowest-risk action that can provide information.
3. Define what will be observed and when.
4. Define a stop condition and an easy opt-out.
5. Check consent, relationships, and practical constraints.
6. Record expected result before action.
7. Later compare expected and actual outcomes.
8. Update, weaken, or preserve the hypothesis without forcing a positive lesson.

A failed or abandoned experiment is information, not a personal failure.
