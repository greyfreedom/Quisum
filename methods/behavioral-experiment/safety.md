# Behavioral Experiment safety

This method inherits `docs/SAFETY_POLICY.md` and may only strengthen it.

## Method-specific constraints

- Do not propose illegal, deceptive, medically risky, financially material, sexually coercive, or confrontation-heavy experiments.
- Do not involve an unaware third party in manipulation or profiling.
- Moderate-risk proposals require explicit user review; acute-risk contexts prohibit experiments.

## Stop routes

Use the crisis route for credible acute risk, refuse covert third-party profiling, and pause when the user asks to stop. Crisis-related content is not eligible for identity inference or ordinary persistence.
